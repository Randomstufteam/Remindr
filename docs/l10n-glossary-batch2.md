## Notes

- Plural variants: cs adds `_Few` (2-4); ro adds `_Few` (0 and 2-19, with "de" in `_Other`); ru and uk add `_Few` and `_Many`. All other languages use One/Other only (id, vi have identical One and Other text).
- Formal address: Finnish, Czech, Romanian, Greek, Russian, Ukrainian, Indonesian use polite/neutral forms; Hungarian and Danish use informal/neutral phrasing, as in Windows 11.
- Hungarian week-range strings reorder the year first (`{2}. {0} – {1}`), matching the Hungarian date convention.
