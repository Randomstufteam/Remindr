Tone follows the earlier batches: de, nl, es, it, pt-BR and the Nordic languages address the reader informally, fr, pt-PT, pl, cs, ro, el and tr politely or neutrally.
"Machine-translated" (the picker tag) and "Help translate" are written as short labels in each language; "Settings_Language_ButtonName" is "Language: {name}. Press to change."
Plural forms added in this pass: Tray_TooltipDue gained Few (cs, ro, pl, ru, uk), Many (pl, ru, uk, ar) and Two (ar, he).
